Infrared Light Therapy

20 minutes

£25
About this treatment

A 20-minute light therapy session. The flexible panel is positioned on the treatment area and you rest while it works. There is no heat, no discomfort, and no downtime afterwards.

Three modes are available, selected based on your skin:

Red + Near-Infrared (633nm + 830nm + 1072nm) supports the appearance of firmer, smoother skin. This is the mode most commonly selected for fine lines, skin texture, and overall radiance.

Blue (465nm) is particularly suited to congested or uneven skin.

Combined (Red + Blue + Near-Infrared) delivers the full range of wavelengths for general skin health.

Results build over a course of sessions. Most clients notice changes in how their skin looks and feels within 4 to 6 weeks of regular treatment, typically two sessions per week. Standalone sessions are also available for maintenance or as an occasional addition to your routine.

The device uses wavelengths that have been widely studied in LED phototherapy research. It is non-invasive, drug-free, and covered under our Balens insurance policy. Please let me know if you are pregnant, have a photosensitive skin condition, or are taking any photosensitising medication including isotretinoin (Roaccutane).
